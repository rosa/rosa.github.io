Hi, I’m Rosa! 👋🏻#

I’m a principal programmer at 37signals, where I’ve worked since 2017 on the Security, Infrastructure and Performance team. I work across Basecamp and HEY and all our legacy apps. I was part of the team that launched HEY, working on email deliverability, spam, security and the app’s overall architecture.

I wrote and maintain Solid Queue, the Active Job backend that ships by default with Rails since 8.0. Since 2023, I’ve represented 37signals on the board of the Rails Foundation.

I love writing code, investigating tricky bugs down rabbit holes, and helping others. I value curiosity, generosity and humility very highly, and I think the three are closely related. Before 37signals, I worked at Plex, BeBanjo and Tuenti, and before that I followed an academic path in theoretical computer science, at Imperial College London and the University of Seville.
