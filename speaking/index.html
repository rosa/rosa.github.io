Speaking

I love tech conferences and attend as many as I can every year, some as part of my job, but most in my own vacation time. Sometimes I speak, too, mostly about what I work on at 37signals and the rabbit holes I fall into along the way. Most talks have a page here with the slides and links to learn more.

I’ve also reviewed talk proposals for RailsConf Philadelphia 2025, EuRuKo 2025, RubyConf Austria 2026, RubyConf Portland 2027 and every Rails World since 2023.
