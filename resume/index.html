Experience#

Principal Engineer, 37signals#

2017 – present · Remote

37signals is the home of Ruby on Rails, which we use across all our products: Basecamp, a project management tool now in its 5th version, and HEY, an email service.

  • I’m part of the Security, Infrastructure and Performance team (SIP), working on all things infrastructure and security in Basecamp and HEY, and supporting our legacy apps.
  • I was instrumental in the launch of HEY, working on email deliverability, spam, resiliency, authentication, security and the overall architecture of the app.
  • I wrote and I’m the principal maintainer of Solid Queue, the Active Job backend included by default in Rails since 8.0.
  • I’ve helped automate our response to our HackerOne bug bounty program with agent skills, and to scan our apps for vulnerabilities.
  • SIP takes on the trickiest, most mysterious bugs, and I love grabbing my magnifying glass and jumping into those investigations, now with the help of a little army of coding agents.
  • I did a two-month internship in the SRE team, and I’ve joined the Product team from time to time to build new features for HEY and Basecamp.

Senior Software Engineer, Plex#

2014 – 2017 · Remote

Backend engineer in the Cloud Services team, in charge of the central service that Plex servers and clients used for user accounts and profiles, premium subscriptions, a registry of media servers with their connection info, server sharing, token-based authentication for clients and sync-list management.

Software Engineer, BeBanjo#

2014 · Remote

In the Projects team, adding features to the backend of BeBanjo’s products and designing and building integrations with our customers’ platforms.

Senior Software Engineer, Tuenti#

2011 – 2014 · Madrid, Spain

Tuenti was then the largest social network in Spain, with 15 million users and 2 petabytes of data delivered per month. I spent most of my time in the Backend Framework team, which built and maintained the data access layer and caching strategies for MySQL and HBase. I later moved to the SOA team, building a framework for the rest of the company’s developers to create new services easily, and a service to manage Tuenti sessions on top of it.
